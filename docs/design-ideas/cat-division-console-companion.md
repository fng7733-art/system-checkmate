# Future design idea: Cat Division console companion

Status: concept archived — not part of the live homepage.

## The idea
A tiny black cat crosses the homepage hero like a living desktop/game icon: a playful nod to 1990s computer interfaces and 16-bit console games (Sega / Nintendo era), rebuilt in SYSTEMCHECKMATE's own visual language.

## Visual explorations
1. **Retro line-art walker** — small dark cat silhouette, fine mint/cyan outline, glowing eyes; walks across the bottom of the hero and briefly looks toward the visitor.
2. **Pixel desktop icon** — chunkier 1990s pixel silhouette, animated legs and tail, small glance.
3. **16-bit console stage** — midnight-blue mini side-scroller with cyan/magenta platform details, stars, tiny HUD reading CAT DIVISION / PLAYER 01, and a pixel cat.

## Design principles if revisited
- Keep it genuinely tiny and secondary; never compete with the page's content or calls to action.
- It must feel handcrafted, not like a generic sticker, emoji, or stock game asset.
- Keep motion calm and occasional; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- No external animation libraries or game engine for such a small detail.
- Test on narrow mobile screens and desktop before deciding.
- The original homepage design remains the preferred live experience unless a future version clearly improves it.

## Decision
The experiment was fun and worth keeping, but the homepage was stronger without it. Archive the idea; do not ship it by default.
